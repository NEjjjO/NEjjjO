### About

I'm Kristóf, founder and CEO of **Shobolinsky**, a space tech startup based in Cluj-Napoca, Romania.

We build hardware for the most unforgiving playgrounds, on Earth or in orbit. Vibration, thermal cycling, zero connectivity and competition judges: we design for all of them.

For the past year I've been deep in **fully offline, low power Edge AI**: inference that runs on the board, next to the sensor, with nothing phoning home. Most of that work ties into research papers and projects around the **European Space Agency (ESA)**.

---

### What I'm building

| Project | What it is |
|:--|:--|
| **REEF** | Modular AI/ML task acceleration for onboard data processing. Presented at EDHPC 2025. |
| **UniCore V0** | ESP32-C3 dev board kit for the UTCN student lab. Native USB, ToF, light and environmental sensors on board. |
| **Lunomyss** | ERC rover. Three CAN networks, custom servo, nav, power and science boards. Minimal COTS, never for main subsystems. |

> **Holding a UniCore right now?** Hi. Yes, we made that. Please don't feed it 12V.
